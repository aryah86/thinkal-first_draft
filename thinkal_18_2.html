How to use a menstrual cup

01

Fold

Press the rim and fold the cup into a tight C or punch-down shape.

02

Insert

Relax, insert at a slight angle, then let it open to form a gentle seal.

03

Remove & rinse

Pinch the base to release the seal, empty, rinse, and reuse.
